## About TME

**Tanger Med Engineering (TME)** is a subsidiary of **Tanger Med Group and NOVEC**, specializing in the planning, design, project management, and asset management of port, maritime, logistics, and industrial infrastructure.

Based in Morocco and active across Africa and internationally, we support infrastructure throughout its lifecycle: from feasibility studies and engineering design to construction supervision, operational support, and maintenance management.

This GitHub organization presents the software and digital engineering dimension of our work, connecting infrastructure expertise with tools that support operational understanding and informed decision-making.

[Learn more about TME](https://www.tme.ma/en/tanger-med-engineering-en/about-us/)

## Engineering Expertise

Our services bring together complementary disciplines to address the technical and operational requirements of infrastructure projects.

| Domain | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ports & Maritime** | Engineering for ports, maritime facilities, and associated infrastructure. |
| **Logistics & Industry** | Planning, design, and construction supervision for logistics platforms, industrial zones, and buildings. |
| **Asset Management** | Maintenance management, asset monitoring, lifecycle performance, and process digitization. |
| **Mobility, Energy & Environment** | Mobility systems, energy infrastructure, renewable energy, and environmental solutions. |
| **Consulting & Training** | Feasibility studies, technical advisory, project support, and professional training. |

## Digital Engineering

Our digital engineering focus is to make infrastructure information easier to access, understand, and use. We bring together spatial context, asset information, and operational workflows to support engineering and maintenance activities.

| Focus Area | Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GIS & BIM** | Connecting geographic information systems and building information models for asset mapping, spatial analysis, and 2D/3D exploration. |
| **Asset & Maintenance Platforms** | Organizing asset information, tracking anomalies, and supporting preventive and corrective maintenance. |
| **Information System Integration** | Connecting asset management platforms with existing information systems and operational workflows. |
| **Dashboards & Performance Monitoring** | Presenting indicators and technical information through interfaces that support analysis and decision-making. |
